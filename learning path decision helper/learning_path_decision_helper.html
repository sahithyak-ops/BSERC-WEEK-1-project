How to learn effectively

A simple approach that works across different subjects.

1

Learn one concept

Use a beginner-friendly tutorial or reference. Focus on understanding rather than rushing.

2

Practise immediately

Try a small exercise without copying the solution. Mistakes help you identify what to revisit.

3

Build something

Apply what you learned in a mini-project that solves a real or everyday problem.

4

Review and improve

Write down what worked, what confused you, and what you want to try next.

Keep your plan realistic

Small, consistent sessions can be more useful than occasional long study sessions.

📅 Plan: Decide what you want to finish before starting each session.
🧠 Recall: Try explaining the concept without looking at your notes.
🛠️ Apply: Use the concept in a small task or project.
🔁 Adapt: If a topic is difficult, spend more time on it instead of blindly following a schedule.
